Quick rules
  • One company per row.
  • Two columns only: company_name and industry_code.
  • Industry code is optional but speeds up classification. See the sponsor directory for the full list of codes.
  • No merged cells, no header notes, no totals row.
  • Save as CSV (UTF-8) before uploading. In Excel: File › Save As › CSV UTF-8.

Send your list to BIZCOM

This checker is a pre-check only. Your list is not submitted until you email it.
Nothing here reaches BIZCOM automatically
The checker compares your list against BIZCOM's records, but it does not submit anything on your behalf. BIZCOM only sees your list once you email it, and approval still comes from them.
Before you email
  • Remove or replace anything flagged prohibited, cooldown or duplicate.
  • Receive approval from Office of Alumni Relations (OAR) before approaching alumni-affiliated companies.
  • Download the CSV from your results, then use Email to BIZCOM below to open a pre-filled draft.
  • Attach the CSV, your sponsorship deck and your outreach email template, then send the draft yourself.
  • Wait for BIZCOM's written approval before contacting any sponsor.

What each status means
  • Clear Previously approved by BIZCOM, safe to approach.
  • Alumni Requires Office of Alumni Relations (OAR) clearance.
  • Prohibited Prohibited per Annex A/B, or the company has closed.
  • Cooldown 30-day outreach cap reached. Please wait for the next cycle.
  • Unverified BIZCOM has not vetted this company before.
  • Duplicate Remove repeating companies from your sponsor list.
Tips
  • One company per row.
  • Include an industry_code column to skip auto-classification.
  • Caps are enforced before submit. Trim if you exceed.
