Current limits

…-day outreach cap

Each sponsor may receive a maximum of … outreach attempts in any rolling …-day window across all SMU Student Activity Groups (SAGs) combined. Once the cap is reached, the sponsor enters a …-day cooldown during which no SAG may approach them.

Event-size caps

The maximum number of distinct sponsors that may be approached per event is determined by the event size:

  • Small event (under 50 attendees): cap of … sponsors.
  • Medium event (50 to 150 attendees): cap of … sponsors.
  • Large event (over 150 attendees): cap of … sponsors.

These caps are cumulative across all submission waves for the same event.
