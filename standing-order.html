SMUSA Sponsorship Standing Order

The policy for student-organised sponsorship at SMU.

1. Purpose and Scope

The Standing Order establishes how Student Activity Groups (SAGs) approach external organisations for sponsorship of SMU-organised events. It exists so SMU clubs present a coordinated face to corporate partners and avoid contradicting each other in outreach.

It applies to all SAGs under SMUSA, including School Constituent Bodies, CCA Constituent Bodies, CCA Clubs, and any group operating under the SMUSA umbrella.

2. Definitions

  • Sponsor: an external organisation contacted to provide cash, in-kind goods, services, prizes, or vouchers.
  • Outreach: any deliberate contact with a sponsor for the purpose of soliciting support (cold email, follow-up, in-person pitch).
  • Master List: the pre-vetted, BIZCOM-maintained database of sponsors eligible for approach.
  • Alumni List: a subset of sponsors owned, founded, or operated by SMU alumni, requiring OAR coordination before approach.
  • Submission: a complete sponsor list submitted to BIZCOM for vetting prior to outreach.

3. Roles and responsibilities

SAGs

SAGs assemble their intended sponsor list, pre-check it through this platform, and submit the cleaned list to the BIZCOM Corporate Relations Secretary for final vetting before any outreach begins.

BIZCOM Corporate Relations Secretary

Maintains the Master List, Prohibited List, and Alumni List. Reviews each submission and confirms approved sponsors back to the SAG within the published turnaround time.

BIZCOM as a whole

Owns enterprise-level partnerships and coordinates with SAGs where there is overlap between BIZCOM's own contacts and a SAG's intended list.

4. Outreach process

  1. Assemble a draft sponsor list with company names and (preferably) industry tags.
  2. Pre-check the list through this platform. Review any flagged rows.
  3. Submit the cleaned list to BIZCOM by email for final approval.
  4. Wait for BIZCOM confirmation before initiating any outreach.
  5. Log each outreach attempt back to BIZCOM so the rolling outreach counter stays accurate for everyone.

5. …-day outreach cap

Each sponsor may receive a maximum of … outreach attempts in any rolling …-day window across all SMU SAGs combined. Once the cap is reached, the sponsor enters a …-day cooldown during which no SAG may approach them.

Rationale: repeated cold outreach to the same company within a short window damages SMU's reputation as a coordinated partner. The cap is enforced advisorily through this platform and definitively at BIZCOM review. Figures on this page are read live from BIZCOM's settings.

6. Event-size caps

The maximum number of distinct sponsors that may be approached per event is determined by the event size:

  • Small event (under 50 attendees): cap of … sponsors.
  • Medium event (50 to 150 attendees): cap of … sponsors.
  • Large event (over 150 attendees): cap of … sponsors.

These caps are cumulative across all submission waves for the same event. Re-classifying an event after submission requires written approval from the Corporate Relations Secretary. Figures on this page are read live from BIZCOM's settings.

7. Master List

The Master List contains sponsors that have been previously approved through BIZCOM vetting. Companies on the Master List are not automatically pre-approved for new events. They still require submission, but they will not be flagged as prohibited, restricted, or alumni-affiliated.

The Master List grows over time. Sponsors approved through one SAG's submission are subsequently added to the Master List, reducing review time for future submissions.

8. Alumni List

Companies owned, founded, or operated by SMU alumni are tracked separately on the Alumni List. Outreach to alumni-affiliated sponsors must be coordinated with the Office of Alumni Relations (OAR) before initiation, regardless of the company's appearance on any other list.

The Alumni List is maintained collaboratively with OAR and is updated as alumni-owned businesses are identified.

  • Office of Alumni Relations (OAR): alumni@smu.edu.sg
  • Office of Advancement (OA): alumnigiving@smu.edu.sg

9. Non-circumvention

SAGs may not circumvent these rules through indirect outreach, including outreach via parent companies, subsidiaries, individual staff contacts, or third parties, to a sponsor that would otherwise be prohibited, on cooldown, or unapproved.

Suspected circumvention should be reported to the Corporate Relations Secretary for investigation.

10. Penalties

Breaches of this standing order may result in: a written warning to the SAG executive committee, suspension of sponsorship privileges for the current academic term, or escalation to the SMUSA Disciplinary Committee for repeat offences.

Penalties are determined case-by-case by the BIZCOM Director in consultation with the SMUSA Executive Committee.

Annex A, prohibited categories

The following organisations, and all associated companies, must not be approached for sponsorship under any circumstances:

  • All Foundations (e.g. Lee Foundation, Shaw Foundation, Tanoto Foundation)
  • SMU Board of Trustees and associated companies: see the sponsor directory for the full list
  • Alcoholic product companies (e.g. Asia Pacific Breweries)
  • Tobacco product companies (e.g. Marlboro)
  • Gaming and betting companies (e.g. Singapore Pools)
  • Sexual product companies (e.g. Durex)
  • SMU Commencement sponsorship companies
  • Insurance companies: no sponsorship, partnership, or collaboration, direct or indirect, monetary or otherwise, including through their subsidiaries, affiliates, intermediaries, representatives, brokers, agents, or any third party acting on their behalf
  • Multi-level marketing companies

Annex B, restricted organisations

The following are organisations BIZCOM is collaborating with directly. CBDs and Student Activity Groups may not approach them on their own. Any other company may still be approached, provided a SAG does not negotiate on behalf of SMUSA or BIZCOM and the company is not offering a corporate discount to all SMU students; if a listed company asks to liaise with SMUSA, refer it to BIZCOM instead.

  • Government entities (e.g. TOTE Board)
  • Banks and financial institutions

SMU Alumni Members are also covered under Annex B. See Section 8, Alumni List for the outreach process and OAR/OA contact details.

Effective date: 1 March 2024. The binding text and all amendments are on the SMUSA Gazette. For clarifications, contact the BIZCOM Corporate Relations Secretary at biz.secretary@smu.edu.sg.
