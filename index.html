Why use it

Less back-and-forth. Cleaner submissions.

Vetting used to be one person, one Excel sheet, late at night. Now it's instant — and built on the same standing order BIZCOM already enforces.

Catch issues before the email

Spot banned, closed, and alumni-affiliated companies the moment you draft your list — not after Secretarial replies three days later.

Respect the 30-day outreach cap

See which companies are already saturated (close to or over their 10-contact rolling cap) so your event isn't the 11th cold ask in a month.

Industry-aware classification

Sponsors are tagged across 15 canonical industries — so you can see at a glance whether your list is well-spread or 200 F&B chains.

How it works

Three steps. Roughly thirty seconds.

1

Upload your CSV

Drop in your sponsor list (CSV or TXT). One column for company name, optionally one for industry. We provide a starter template.

2

We cross-check instantly

Every company is matched (exact and fuzzy) against the master list, banned list, alumni database, and current outreach counts.

3

Download or email

Download the annotated CSV with status, reason, and recommendation for each row — or email it straight to BIZCOM.
