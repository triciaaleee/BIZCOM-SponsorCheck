How to use it

Your workflow, end to end

Five steps, from your raw sponsor list to a BIZCOM-ready submission.

  1. 1

    Read the standing order

    Start with the SMUSA Sponsorship Standing Order. It sets out which companies you cannot approach, the 30-day outreach cap, and the per-event sponsor limits.

  2. 2

    Prepare your sponsor list

    Check the sponsor directory for the 15 industry tags and the list of companies you should avoid. Then head to the checker and download the Excel template to start filling in your sponsors.

  3. 3

    Save your file as CSV

    In Excel: File › Save As › CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited). The checker only accepts CSV files.

  4. 4

    Review the flags and edit accordingly

    Upload the CSV to the checker. Remove or replace any company flagged as prohibited, alumni, or cooldown before sending the list to BIZCOM.

  5. 5

    Email the cleaned list to BIZCOM

    Use the email template on the checker to submit. Attach your annotated sponsor CSV, your sponsorship deck, and your outreach email template before sending.

What the checker does

Built around the standing order

The checker enforces the rules students usually miss.

Prohibited and closed companies

Cross-checks every name against Annex A and B of SMUSA's Sponsorship Standing Order, and companies that have closed.

Alumni-affiliated businesses

Flags companies owned by SMU alumni, which require Office of Alumni Relations (OAR) clearance before any outreach.

30-day outreach cap

Warns when a sponsor is close to or already over the 10-contact rolling cap.

Industry classification

Tags every company with one of 15 industry categories so you can see whether your list is well-spread.

Download annotated CSV

Export the result with status, reason, and recommendation per row. Use it as your BIZCOM submission.

Email to BIZCOM directly

Open a pre-filled email to the Corporate Relations Secretary with your event details and the cleaned list attached.
